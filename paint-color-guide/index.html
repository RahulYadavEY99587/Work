Luxury Finish Rules

Follow these for a premium result

1. One Bold Wall Only

Thunder Bay 2420 on the TV wall is your only dark accent. Every other wall stays light. This is the #1 luxury rule.

2. Ceiling = White Crystal

Code 2484 on every ceiling. Non-negotiable. Makes rooms feel taller and more expensive.

3. Consistent Trim

Off White 333 on all frames + Mahogany 517 on all doors. Same across every room.

4. Buy One Batch

Get all paint mixed in one go from the same dealer to avoid shade variation between cans.

5. Test Before Full Paint

Buy 200ml sample of Thunder Bay 2420. Paint a 2×2 ft patch. Check morning + evening light.

6. Gallery = Living Room

Honey Cream 2036 in the gallery connects spaces seamlessly. No different color needed.
